Informed consent to the practice of Yoga

The practice of Yoga is a physical and educational activity that engages body and mind. Although it is taught with all due care and professionalism, it cannot be entirely free of risk.

By signing this document, I declare:

  • that I have completed, sincerely, the confidential medical questionnaire provided by SHIDO MIND YOGA;
  • that I have disclosed all information relating to my state of health;
  • that I undertake to inform SHIDO MIND YOGA in writing of any change in my state of health, any new treatment, injury, pregnancy, surgical procedure or medical contraindication that may affect my practice;
  • that I understand that the teaching offered constitutes neither a medical act, nor a diagnosis, nor a treatment, and in no way replaces the follow-up of my doctor or another health professional;
  • that I practise freely, within my physical capacities and in accordance with the instructions given by the teacher;
  • that I will immediately stop my practice and inform the teacher in the event of pain, discomfort, dizziness, unusual breathlessness or any other concerning symptom;
  • that I acknowledge that the adaptations proposed by the teacher are intended to improve the safety of my practice, without being able to eliminate entirely the risks inherent in any physical activity.

I acknowledge that SHIDO MIND YOGA cannot be held liable for the consequences of any omission, inaccurate or incomplete statement concerning my state of health, any failure to follow safety instructions, or the voluntary continuation of practice despite pain, discomfort or a known medical contraindication.

I confirm that I have had the opportunity to ask any useful questions before beginning my practice, and that I take part in full knowledge of the facts.
