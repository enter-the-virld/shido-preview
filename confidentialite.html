Privacy policy.

Last updated: 21 August 2026

At SHIDO® | House of Yoga Education, we attach great importance to the confidentiality and protection of your personal data.

This policy explains what data we may collect, why we use it and how we protect it.

1.Data controller

The data controller is: SHIDO® | House of Yoga Education, Casablanca, Morocco. Email: contact@shidomindyoga.com

2.The data we collect

Depending on how you use our site and services, we may collect:

  • your first and last name;
  • your email address and telephone number;
  • the information required for your registration, booking or membership;
  • information relating to your exchanges with SHIDO;
  • the information required for payment;
  • certain technical information relating to your browsing of our site.

In the context of certain practices, we may also collect health-related information, only where it is necessary for the safety and adaptation of the practice.

3.Why do we use your data?

Your data is used only where necessary to:

  • manage your registrations, bookings and memberships;
  • respond to your requests;
  • ensure the smooth running of your practice;
  • process payments;
  • send you information about your sessions;
  • improve and secure our site and services;
  • send you our news and communications, where you have chosen to receive them.

We neither sell nor rent your personal data to third parties.

4.Health data

Health-related information is treated confidentially and solely for the purpose of ensuring a safe, adapted practice. It is accessible only to those who need to know it in the context of your support. SHIDO complies with the obligations applicable to sensitive personal data, in accordance with Moroccan regulations.

5.Service providers

To run our activity, certain data may be processed by trusted service providers, in particular for:

  • hosting of the site;
  • booking and membership management;
  • payments;
  • sending emails;
  • site analytics, where such tools are used.

These providers access only the data necessary for their services.

6.Cookies

Our site may use cookies necessary for its operation and, where applicable, cookies intended to measure the audience or improve your experience. You can manage cookies from your browser settings or, where offered, from our cookie management tool.

7.Retention and security

We keep your data only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or for the period required by regulation. We implement reasonable measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, use or disclosure.

8.Your rights

In accordance with applicable Moroccan regulations, you may in particular request access to your personal data, their rectification, or object to certain processing, under the conditions provided by law. To exercise your rights, contact us at: contact@shidomindyoga.com

9.Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in our services or in regulation. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.
